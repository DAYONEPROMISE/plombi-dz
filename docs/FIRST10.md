# PlombiDZ — First 10 customers plan (Alger)

1–2. **Two neighbourhood plumbers (tester champions).** Install APK by
Bluetooth/WhatsApp, watch one real sizing + one fiche on site, fix wording.
3–4. **Quincaillerie plumbing counters (Alger, Bab Ezzouar).** Demo at the
counter; counter staff recommend to artisan buyers; leave QR-to-APK card.
5–6. **Two small plumbing crews (villa sites).** Fiche+PDF replaces paper
notebook; collect before/after time feedback.
7. **One CFPA / vocational plumbing instructor.** Pedagogical use of the
DTU 60.11 module; credibility + student installs.
8. **One building-materials wholesaler.** Pre-install pitch on demo phones.
9–10. **Two Facebook/Instagram Algerian bricolage pages.** Short Arabic
screen-record demo; APK link in comments.

Success bar for v1.1: 10 active plumbers, 50+ fiches created, 0 crash
reports, 3 quoted testimonials. Then decide Play release.
